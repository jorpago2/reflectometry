Model assumptions and scope

Normal incidence; homogeneous isotropic coherent layers; finite phase-incoherent substrate with uniform n + ik, Beer–Lambert attenuation, and incoherent rear-surface returns. Cauchy–Urbach is phenomenological, Sellmeier assumes transparency, EMA assumes subwavelength isotropic constituents, and the five-knot KK spline is bandwidth limited. Surface roughness, gradients, anisotropy, scattering, oblique incidence, and substrate-index dispersion are not included.
