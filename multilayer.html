Model assumptions and scope

Normal incidence; homogeneous isotropic layers; coherent propagation inside the stack; optically thick substrate with incoherent rear-surface returns; constant real substrate index. Surface roughness, gradients, anisotropy, scattering, oblique incidence, and finite substrate absorption are not included.
